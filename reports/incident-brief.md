# Incident Brief

## Incident ID
FIN-INC-001

## Organization
Fictional Small Fintech Company

## Incident Type
Unauthorized Transactions / Compromised Customer Accounts

## Initial Detection
A customer reports unauthorized transactions from their account.

## Investigation Objective
Determine:
- The likely entry point
- The attack path
- The security weakness that enabled the incident
- The affected accounts and transactions
- Whether external attacker or insider activity is more plausible
- How the incident could have been detected earlier

## Evidence Sources
- Authentication logs
- Transaction logs
- Account activity logs
- Network/security logs

## Scope
The investigation focuses on compromised customer accounts,
unauthorized transaction activity, relevant authentication and
transaction logs, and security controls directly related to the incident.

## Out of Scope
- Complete fintech security architecture
- Unrelated applications
- Unrelated business processes
- Production systems
- Real customer or financial data

## Initial Hypothesis
Unknown. The investigation will use the available evidence
to determine the most plausible attack path.
