# Investigation Questions

## Primary Question

How were the fintech customer accounts compromised, and how did
the compromise lead to unauthorized transactions?

## Supporting Questions

1. What was the likely entry point into the affected customer accounts?

2. What evidence indicates that the accounts were compromised?

3. What suspicious authentication activity occurred before the
   unauthorized transactions?

4. Which transactions were unauthorized?

5. What account activity occurred after the suspected compromise?

6. What evidence connects the authentication activity, account
   changes, and unauthorized transactions?

7. What security weakness or vulnerability enabled the incident?

8. Is external attacker activity or insider activity more plausible
   based on the available evidence?

9. How was the incident detected?

10. What controls could have detected or prevented the compromise
    earlier?

11. What containment and remediation actions should be recommended?
